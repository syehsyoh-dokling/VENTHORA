# Live run on the Airwallex sandbox, 14–15 Nov 2026

What happened on the live demo company (Nusantara Trading Pte Ltd, `app-venthora.muwahid.org`) when the 72-hour
scenario was run with sandbox money. Every figure below was read from the running service; nothing here is simulated
by VENTHORA itself. Times are UTC. The defects the run exposed are listed too, with what was done about each.

## The scenario

17 vendors and their bills in six currencies (SGD, IDR, GBP, EUR, JPY, USD) due inside 72 hours, against a book of
USD 500,000 ring-fenced from the wallet and a USD 50,000 reserve floor. Every vendor was new: bank details checked at
Airwallex, a call-back recorded on the registered number, and the first payment to each approved by the FCO.

fitted, in priority order, and deferred the rest. The reserve was never crossed.

## The deposit: the plan changes by itself

| Step | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| A customer deposit of USD 150,000 from Acme Retail Ltd settles (15 Nov 10:31) | book USD 51,331.35 | book USD 201,331.35 |
| The deposit is credited to the book | once (`book_deposit_credited` in the audit chain) | reading the plan again credits nothing more |
| Pacific Health Insurance, JPY 18,000,000 | DEFER: no room above the reserve | CONVERT AND PAY |

Only a settled deposit from a registered customer is credited; the two earlier top-ups from the company's own bank
were not, because how much of the wallet the book may use is the FCO's decision.

## Controls that stopped a payment, as designed

| What | Control | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 17 new vendors | Payee lifecycle: no payment before a call-back on the registered number | Nothing was paid until each call-back was recorded |
| 13 vendors with incomplete bank details | Bank check at Airwallex before details are kept | Refused with the fields named; paid only after the details passed |
| Every first payment | First payment to a newly verified payee needs a person | Each one approved by the FCO |
| Straits Line, Meridian, Lumen Office, CloudDesk | Budget allocation at execution | Refused until the Operations line had room; CloudDesk (GBP 2,400) is still refused |
| `REQ-SCF-118`, AUD 2,500 | No budget allocation covers its charge code | Left out of the plan with the reason |
| Indonesian payroll in one transfer | The bank's own limit | Refused by Airwallex; no money moved; split and paid |

## Defects the run exposed, and what was done

The suite was green before each of these was found; they appear only with live money, new payees and mixed currencies.

| Found | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The plan did not check the budget line or the bank's transfer limit | Payments shown as payable were refused at execution | `plan_gates.py`: refused before planning, with the reason; the limit is learned from the bank's refusal |
| A conversion whose payout then failed left EUR 953.97 unused | Later EUR payments bought euros again | The book's own unspent currency is counted and used first |
| A customer deposit did not reach the book | The plan after a deposit was the same as before | `book_funding.py`: a settled customer deposit is credited once |
| Every write under `/v1/doa` was the FCO's alone | HR and finance managers could not do their own steps | `access.py`: settings of the delegation separated from the work it governs |
| A paid request never left the planner's queue | Paid invoices held 11 of the 12 places a plan proves | The intake store attaches the payment to every request it returns |
| The FX cross-check allowed one cent per conversion | A correct plan with JPY 18,000,000 was blocked over 2.25 cents of rate rounding | The tolerance grows with the amount bought |

No payment was made twice at any point: the one-payout-per-invoice lock held throughout.

## What is still open on the live company

- Pacific Health (JPY 18,000,000) is planned but not yet paid: it waits for the FX cross-check fix to be deployed.
- CloudDesk (GBP 2,400) is refused by the Operations budget, which has USD 1,097.49 left.
- EUR 953.97 bought earlier is in the book and will be used by the next EUR payment.
- The invoice simulation left about 70 small invoices in the queue; they are payable requests, not part of the scenario.
