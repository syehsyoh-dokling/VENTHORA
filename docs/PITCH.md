# VENTHORA, Evidence-Gated Treasury Agent

**Role:** Team Lead / AI Engineer (Agentic) / Solutions Architect  |  Autonomous treasury and payment-ops agent on Airwallex (Agentic Banking Hackathon 2026, Starter Kits 01 + 03)

VENTHORA lets a finance team hand real money movements to an agent without handing it the account. When cash is short and obligations in three currencies fall due within 72 hours, it decides what to fund, convert, defer or escalate, converts FX against single-use quotes, pays through Airwallex, confirms every deposit as settled before counting it, reconciles the wallet to the cent against a double-entry ledger per book, and learns the real fees for the next cycle. It acts only inside a mandate it can prove: the period's budget per line, a reserve floor with an FX stress buffer, limits on a single payout, a payee per period, a first payment, daily and weekly outflow, open FX positions and the day's conversions, and approvals bound to the exact terms the approver saw, from as many different people as the approval matrix requires and never from the requester. Evidence from the inbox, read by a local language model, counts only when it can be quoted verbatim from a dated document; payment requests from external mailboxes (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) are trusted only by their registered address, and a payee whose bank details change cannot be paid by anyone until a finance manager calls back on the registered number and a cooling period has passed. When a supplier transfer comes back, it waits, replaces under a duplicate lock with a new request id, or escalates, and closes the incident only once the original's fate is on file. On the real Airwallex sandbox it completed eighteen recorded cycles and an eight-step end-to-end suite (8 / 8); against 322 hand-derived ground-truth cases in twelve suites it answers 322 / 322 in-process, through the HTTP API and through the dashboard form; across a 136-variant scenario matrix from zero cash to prompt injection and 429 storms it satisfies every invariant; its policy evaluates identically in Python and in Rego on 10,618 inputs under a real OPA; and a plan with its 72-hour probabilistic reserve proof and seven counterfactuals takes about 5 ms in-process and about 100 ms through the API, live balance check included. The result is an agent that moves money faster than a treasury desk and refuses, with a written reason, whenever the proof fails.

---

An AI treasury agent that must prove a payment is safe before it is allowed to make it. It reads the book, the policy and the inbox ("Acme's USD 10,000 will be five days late", "Lumen changed its bank account", "final notice: invoice 60 days past due"), plans the next 72 hours with an exact optimizer, proves the plan against invariants, policy, live balances and a simulated distribution of outcomes, executes on Airwallex with idempotent operations, and reconciles what actually moved. On the organizers' own failure modes (fees that break a reserve, receipts that slip, lookalike payees, stale quotes, lost responses, CANCELLED transfers nobody cancelled, the same invoice twice) it does the right thing 46 / 46 times by hand-derived ground truth, and 7 of those were reproduced on the real rail. Every control added since was proven the same way: ground truth on three paths, then a cycle on the real sandbox.

**Architecture.** Four engines, one gate, nothing moves on a narrative. *Verity* admits only evidence it can cite: every claim a model makes about a receipt or a payee must be located verbatim in a dated document; it rejects duplicates per invoice and per instalment, runs a three-way match against the contract, ages every payable (buckets per payee, late interest accrued and projected, dunning level from the supplier's own reminders), and escalates any payee that is unverified, changed, suspended or outside the book's tenant. *Horizon* turns ECB history into a joint FX stress buffer (the original M0 scenario engine) and a 72-hour discrete-event simulation of receipts, due dates and shocks that yields a breach probability for the plan and for every counterfactual (vectorised, 1–4 million path-events per second over the loop). *Theorem* is an exact CP-SAT optimizer with an independent second candidate that must agree, hard caps per budget line, and a worked frame (given / asked / formula / answer) for every decision; what no longer fits a line becomes a budget request with the exact shortfall. *ProofCore* is the gate: structural invariants, the reserve rule, FX-leg netting, the budget mandate, limits and velocity, FX exposure, the payee lifecycle, aging, tenancy and the approval matrix, all in Python mirrored one-to-one in Rego (OPA), plus live Airwallex balances and an optional TigerBeetle ledger; any failure blocks, any limit names the person and the number of different people who must approve, bound to a terms digest. The executor checks the book's own ledger before money moves, claims every payout, persists request ids, recovers lost responses by request id rather than retrying, refuses stale states, production URLs and a book locked by an open reconciliation case, re-reads the payee's live record, accepts only the book's named approvers, learns real fees and reconciles the wallet against every book's cash lines to the cent. Reporting follows the admin's cut-off (day, time, timezone), books each payment to the period of its payment instant, reports invoices paid in instalments, and consolidates every currency into one reporting currency at recorded closing rates. Everything is logged in a hash-chained audit store, and a dashboard shows the plan and its counterfactual proof, the budget pipeline, the book ledgers, the payees and their lifecycle, the tenants and their books, reporting periods, intake, executions and incidents, with a submission form that measures each case against ground truth.

**Stack:** Airwallex REST (balances, quotes, conversions, beneficiaries schema → validate → create, transfers, deposits, simulation) · Python 3.13 / FastAPI / pydantic v2 / httpx · OR-Tools CP-SAT · numpy · Open Policy Agent (Rego) · SQLite (execution store with per-book double-entry journal, payee registry, intake queue, hash-chained audit) · local Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on an OpenAI-compatible server with a deterministic lexicon fallback · the original Theorem, Horizon M0 and Verity engines vendored with SHA-256 provenance · TigerBeetle (optional ledger) · 648 tests, ruff, a 136-variant scenario matrix, 322 ground-truth cases in 12 suites, 18 real-sandbox cycles. All decision logic runs 100% local; only the money moves through Airwallex.
