# VENTHORA, Evidence-Gated Treasury Agent

**Role:** Team Lead / AI Engineer (Agentic) / Solutions Architect  |  Autonomous treasury and payment-ops agent on Airwallex (Agentic Banking Hackathon 2026, Starter Kits 01 + 03)

VENTHORA lets a finance team hand real money movements to an agent without handing it the account. When cash is short and obligations in three currencies fall due within 72 hours, it decides what to fund, convert, defer or escalate, converts FX against single-use quotes, pays through Airwallex, confirms every deposit as settled before counting it, reconciles the wallet to the cent and learns the real fees for the next cycle. It only acts within a mandate it can prove: the period's budget per line, a reserve floor with an FX stress buffer, an autonomous FX limit that shrinks as receipt forecasts weaken, and approvals bound to the exact terms the approver saw. Evidence from the inbox, read by a local language model, counts only when it can be quoted verbatim from a dated document; payment requests from external mailboxes (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) are trusted only by their registered address, never by a display name or by anything the email says. When a supplier transfer comes back, it waits, replaces under a duplicate lock with a new request id, or escalates, and closes the incident only once the original's fate is on file. On the real Airwallex sandbox it completed nine recorded cycles and an eight-step end-to-end suite (8 / 8); against 120 hand-derived ground-truth cases it answers 120 / 120 on three paths (in-process, HTTP, dashboard form); across a 136-variant scenario matrix from zero cash to prompt injection and 429 storms it satisfies every invariant; its policy evaluates identically in Python and in Rego on 9,518 inputs under a real OPA; and a plan with its 72-hour probabilistic reserve proof and seven counterfactuals takes about 13 ms. The result is an agent that moves money faster than a treasury desk and refuses, with a written reason, whenever the proof fails.

An AI treasury agent that must prove a payment is safe before it is allowed to make it. It reads the book, the policy and the inbox ("Acme's USD 10,000 will be five days late", "Lumen changed its bank account"), plans the next 72 hours with an exact optimizer, proves the plan against invariants, policy, live balances and a simulated distribution of outcomes, executes on Airwallex with idempotent operations, and reconciles what actually moved. On the organizers' own failure modes — fees that break a reserve, receipts that slip, lookalike payees, stale quotes, lost responses, CANCELLED transfers that nobody cancelled, the same invoice twice — it does the right thing 46 / 46 times by hand-derived ground truth, and 7 of those were reproduced on the real rail.

**Architecture.** Four engines, one gate, nothing moves on a narrative. *Verity* admits only evidence it can cite: every claim a model makes about a receipt or a payee must be located verbatim in a dated document, with embargoed and invented claims rejected. *Horizon* turns ECB history into a joint FX stress buffer (the original M0 scenario engine) and a 72-hour discrete-event simulation of receipts, due dates and shocks that yields a breach probability for the plan and for every counterfactual (vectorised, 1–9 million path-events per second, measured over the loop only). *Theorem* is an exact CP-SAT optimizer with an independent second candidate that must agree, hard caps per budget line, and a worked frame (given / asked / formula / answer) for every decision; what no longer fits a line becomes a budget request stating the exact shortfall. *ProofCore* is the gate: structural invariants, the reserve rule, FX-leg netting, the policy in Python mirrored one-to-one in Rego (OPA), live Airwallex balances, an optional TigerBeetle ledger, and the budget mandate; any failure blocks, any limit requires a named person whose approval is bound to a terms digest. The executor claims every payout before money moves, persists request ids, recovers lost responses by request id rather than retrying, refuses stale states and production URLs, learns real fees and reconciles the managed budget to the cent. Incremental recalculation re-opens only the decisions new information touches and reports a decision diff; an AI challenger that proposes a wrong plan is blocked by the same gate that approves a right one. Everything is logged in a hash-chained audit store, and a dashboard shows the plan, the counterfactual proof, the budget pipeline (plan · realized · committed · available · minus), the intake queue, executions and incidents, with a submission form that measures each request against ground truth.

**Stack:** Airwallex REST (balances, quotes, conversions, beneficiaries schema → validate → create, transfers, deposits, simulation) · Python 3.13 / FastAPI / pydantic v2 / httpx · OR-Tools CP-SAT · numpy · Open Policy Agent (Rego) · SQLite (execution store, intake queue, hash-chained audit) · local Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on an OpenAI-compatible server with a deterministic lexicon fallback · the original Theorem, Horizon M0 and Verity engines vendored with SHA-256 provenance · TigerBeetle (optional ledger) · 417 tests, ruff, a 136-variant scenario matrix, 120 ground-truth cases, 9 real-sandbox cycles. All decision logic runs 100% local; only the money moves through Airwallex.
