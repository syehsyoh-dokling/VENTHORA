Airwallex Cards

Staff cards under the same mandate as transfers. Each person can have one Visa virtual card from Airwallex Issuing, closed until an approved advance opens it: only for the purposes the Finance Director ticks, only inside its window and amount, and only at the merchants allowed. Every charge is asked first (remote authorization) and decided from the mandate and the card program alone. VENTHORA keeps the card id and the masked number, never the full number.
loading…

Card Authorization

Airwallex asks VENTHORA before every card charge goes (remote authorization): within 2.5 seconds VENTHORA answers AUTHORIZED or DECLINED from the mandate alone: a card it issued and has not frozen, the amount inside the card's limits and the mandate's single payout limit, the vendor's merchant, a charge not decided before. The request is signed with a shared secret Airwallex returns once; it is kept here and never shown. If VENTHORA does not answer in time, Airwallex applies the default action (set it to DECLINED).

Visa Agent Identity (TAP)

VENTHORA proves to a merchant that a request comes from it (Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol). Each request to a merchant is signed with the agent's Ed25519 key (RFC 9421 HTTP Message Signatures, as Visa's sample agent does): agent-browser-auth to browse, agent-payer-auth to pay. The merchant checks the signature with the agent's public key, published at /.well-known/venthora-agent.json on the gateway. The private key stays on this machine.

Alta · testnet anchoring

VENTHORA's records, provable on a public chain. VENTHORA keeps two kinds of hash chain: the audit log of every decision and execution, and one chain per contract (Reporting → Contract blockchain). Anchor now writes the SHA-256 digest of their latest hashes on Alta Testnet (chain 63825) in one transaction, from VENTHORA's own testnet address; gas is a fraction of a cent in test USDZ and the transaction is final in about a second. Anyone can open it on the explorer and recompute the digest from VENTHORA's records: a decision, invoice or payment changed after the anchor no longer matches. Money still moves on Airwallex; Alta only holds the proof.

Visa Intelligent Commerce

How to get the key · Visa's credentials are issued by Visa, not by an API
  1. Create a project on the Visa Developer Center and add the Visa Intelligent Commerce and X-Pay APIs.
  2. In the project's Credentials, copy the API key and the shared secret (X-Pay token); download the encryption key if the project uses one.
  3. Enter them below and press Test. For agent card payments through Airwallex, VIC comes with Airwallex's Airi: ask the Airwallex team to allowlist the account for airi-cli.
The Visa Developer project VENTHORA calls. The sandbox project's API key and shared secret (X-Pay token) test the connection; Visa's Intelligent Commerce onboarding adds the consumer and the enrolled card token. With them, VENTHORA turns its mandate for a card-billed vendor into Visa's purchase instruction (a decline threshold, the preferred merchant, the category, an end date), so Visa itself holds the agent to it. Credentials are kept write-only.

Local Rails Indonesia · Xendit and Midtrans

Everything the other menus cannot do in Indonesia, and nothing they already do. Airwallex pays Indonesian bank accounts, cards spend at Visa merchants and Alta keeps the record, but none of them pays an e-wallet, says whose account it is, or collects rupiah by QRIS, virtual account or cash. Two local providers close those gaps: Xendit for money going out and the holder's name, Midtrans for money coming in. Keys are write-only and sandbox only.

T:0 books · journal posting and verification

Each ledger entry becomes one manual journal entry in T:0, once only (VENTHORA's note and T:0's idempotency key). Amounts in other currencies are booked in T:0's currency (USD) at the ECB rate of the day, with the original amount kept. Every posted entry is read back from T:0 and compared: debits = credits, each line's account, side and amount, the total, the currency, the date and the VENTHORA reference.

FX & transfer costs

What it cost to change currency and send the money. One line per Airwallex transaction (its transaction code and value date), tied to the payment, the vendor, the invoice and the budget line it served; every cost in the main currency. Total cost = exchange spread + payout fee. The spread is what was sold minus what the bought amount is worth at the mid-market rate of the Airwallex quote; it is never guessed: without that rate it shows as not measured.

Vendor Sourcing

Three quotations for every purchase request. Pick a purchase request from Intake: the agent looks for vendors that sell what it asks for, among the registered vendors and online (web search by the AI model; the demo directory when the model cannot search). Nothing is sent by finding: you choose the vendors and send the request for quotation. Each vendor gets its own form link: company details, bank details, a checklist of every specification with a remark, the brand offered, the price, delivery time, validity and the quotation document. Quotations arrive in Quotation.
loading…

Shortlist

At most three vendors per purchase request, best first, each with its justification. Eligible quotations (every specification, the brand, currency, quantity and validity) rank by total price; the first is recommended. Select raises the PO, sends the winner the purchase order with its own form for the bank details, thanks the others, and registers the PO on the contract blockchain.
loading…

Quotation

Every quotation, one row per vendor, grouped by purchase request. The recommendation keeps only quotations that meet every specification, offer the requested brand when the request names one (the brand is then locked), cover the quantity and are still valid, and picks the cheapest. With an AI model set up, Check remarks with the model reads every remark against its specification and marks a "complies" the remark contradicts as not met. A person then approves one quotation: the recommended one, or another with the reason. The approval registers the vendor (no payment before the call-back), raises the PO, marks the request ordered and queues the PO and the regret letters.

Contract blockchain

Every contract is its own chain. Purchase orders and consultant contracts: block 0 seals what was agreed (supplier, currency, value, terms, charge code, dates, LOE); every invoice and every payment against the contract (with its Airwallex transaction code) is the next block. Each block carries the SHA-256 hash of the block before it, so changing anything in the past breaks every hash after it. The chain is sealed each time this report is read, and the seal is written to the audit log's own hash chain. Next to the chain: the checks a finance controller runs on each contract.

Budget allocation

Which budget a payment draws on. Every payment carries a charge code OFFICE.PROJECT.ACCOUNT. The hierarchy is the organisation's: head office, country office, branch, unit or department. A budget given only at the top covers every office and unit under it; a budget given to a lower level covers that level only, and the nearest one governs. Every payment is checked against it again at execution, and never beyond the actual balance.

This page sets the organisation's financial calendar. The fiscal year and its quarters decide how every budget, allocation and spending report is grouped; the active budget period is the allocation each payment is checked against before VENTHORA pays it; the cut-off decides which month a transaction is reported in. Set it once at the start of the fiscal year: every payment, report and Delegation of Authority run uses it. How to use it: choose the month the fiscal year starts and the budget period, check the quarters, set the cut-off, save.

2 · Reporting cut-offwhich month a transaction is reported in

3 · Budget controlwhat happens when an allocation is used up

Limited: a payment charged to an office, project or account whose allocation for the active period is used up waits for a person.

Every day at this time VENTHORA re-reads the connected sources and recalculates what each allocation has spent in the active period.

Why every office, branch and unit is here
This tree is the organisation as VENTHORA sees it, and every payment is placed in it before it is paid:
Charge codes. Each payment carries OFFICE.PROJECT.ACCOUNT; the office part is the cost centre of a unit here. A code whose cost centre is not here, or not active, is never paid.
Budgets. Budget allocation follows this tree: a budget given to the head office covers every office under it; one given to a branch or unit covers that branch or unit only (Payment → Budget allocation).
Who approves. People belong to a unit (People and roles). An exception is routed to the approvers in the unit's chain whose approval limit covers it, nearest first; nobody approves outside their chain.
How much the agent pays alone. The payment limit of a unit (or of the nearest unit above it that sets one) is what VENTHORA may pay on its own for spending charged to that unit; above it a person approves.
Local currency and time zone. The currency an office is budgeted and reports in, and its local time; the reporting cut-off itself is one for the organisation (FCO Authorize → Period, cut-off & sync).
Reports and segregation. Spending, payment reports and allocations are added up per office, branch and unit, and an admin of one office manages only that office and the units under it.

Upload datasetany file, any layout: VENTHORA's agent reads it and files the data where it belongs

1 · What you do
  1. Choose one or more files: Excel, CSV, JSON, Word, PDF, HTML, an e-mail or plain text.
  2. Leave Auto-detect on, or pick the kind of data.
  3. Press Read the file(s), check the column matches, press Preview.
  4. Press Commit when the preview looks right.
2 · What VENTHORA does
  • Reads the file and decides what it holds: vendors, people, POs, budgets…
  • Matches each column to a VENTHORA field and cleans the values (amounts, dates, IBANs).
  • Preview: shows what would be created, updated or rejected, and why. Nothing is written yet.
  • Commit: writes the records through the same checks as entering them by hand.
3 · The result
  • Each record lands on its own page (Payees, Offices, Purchase orders…), with a link after Commit.
  • A row that fails a check is rejected with the reason; the rest go in.
  • A vendor's new or changed bank details still wait for a call-back before any payment.
  • Every commit is in the audit log: who, which file, how many records.
4 · Why use it
  • You: no API, no reformatting, no typing records one by one; the file as your old system exports it.
  • VENTHORA: starts on the company's real data instead of placeholders, checked on the way in.
5 · Limits and who reads the file
  • checking the AI model…
  • A one-time import, not a live link: upload again when the data changes, or use App Integration for a sync.
  • Photos, scans and old .xls files cannot be read; upload the PDF with text, .xlsx or CSV.
  • Up to 15 MB and 5,000 rows per file; Excel reads the first sheet that has data.
  • The file itself is not stored: it is held in memory until Commit, then only the committed records are kept.

How the model is used

  • It reads emails, PDFs and the inbox, and proposes values with the words it read them from.
  • Code keeps a value only if that quote is in the source and the value is inside it; otherwise it is dropped.
  • When the model and the rules disagree, a person decides. It never sets an amount or moves money.

Delegation of Authority (DoA)

What the FCO delegates to VENTHORA's agent, by kind of payment. Internal payments are the organisation's own recurring obligations: the FCO sets one standing payment instruction per cost category, and on the pay date the agent pays on its own what the instruction allows. External payments are invoices from vendors and short-term specialists. Anything outside the delegation is prepared for a person; some things are never executed. Every payment still passes the treasury limits and controls.
loading…
The mandate is the authority the finance team gives VENTHORA's agent. Inside it the agent checks, plans and pays on its own; outside it, it stops and asks a person. It has three parts: the checks every request must pass before anything is payable, the permissions and safeguards with their limits, and the approval policy for everything else. Every run on the Operations page starts from this standing mandate and records exactly what was granted. One role holds the authority to change it.
