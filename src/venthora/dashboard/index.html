FX & transfer costs

What it cost to move the money, in the base currency. Two costs, never mixed: the exchange spread (what was sold minus what the bought amount is worth at the mid-market rate of the Airwallex quote), for a conversion booked before a payout (EUR, GBP, SGD, ...) and for a payout Airwallex converted inside the transfer because it cannot be funded in its own currency (IDR, INR, MYR, ...); and the payout fee Airwallex charged. Both are set against what the period converted and paid. A spread is never guessed: without the mid-market rate it shows as not measured.

Everyone VENTHORA pays, routes to, or takes an approval from
Every employee of the head office, a country office, a branch or a unit belongs here, with what the payment automations read:
Salary. The monthly net pay, its currency and the charge code it is booked to fill the payroll register line of each person (VENTHORA prepares the payroll from it every period; Reporting → Payroll shows each line); the contract start and end decide whether a salary is due at all.
Their own bank account. Salary, travel claims and cash advances go only to the person's own registered account, verified by a call-back like any beneficiary; without one, nothing is paid to them on the agent's authority.
Benefits. The fringe benefits the person is enrolled in (insurance, pension, social security…), from the list in Delegation of Authority → 02.
Approvals. The role decides what the person may do in VENTHORA; the unit and the approval limit decide which exceptions they approve; nobody approves their own request.
Senders. The person's email is the mailbox their claims, travel requests and instructions are accepted from.
Employment. Staff number, job title and employment type (permanent, fixed-term, expatriate, part-time, intern) identify the person on the register and in the reports.

Budget allocation

Which budget a payment draws on. Every payment carries a charge code OFFICE.PROJECT.ACCOUNT. The hierarchy is the organisation's: head office, country office, branch, unit or department. A budget given only at the top covers every office and unit under it; a budget given to a lower level covers that level only, and the nearest one governs. Every payment is checked against it again at execution, and never beyond the actual balance.

This page sets the organisation's financial calendar. The fiscal year and its quarters decide how every budget, allocation and spending report is grouped; the active budget period is the allocation each payment is checked against before VENTHORA pays it; the cut-off decides which month a transaction is reported in. Set it once at the start of the fiscal year: every payment, report and Delegation of Authority run uses it. How to use it: choose the month the fiscal year starts and the budget period, check the quarters, set the cut-off, save.

1 · Fiscal year and quartershow budgets, allocations and spending are grouped

The active budget period is the slice of the budget each payment draws on: an annual budget, or the quarter, half or month it falls in.

2 · Reporting cut-offwhich month a transaction is reported in

3 · Budget controlwhat happens when an allocation is used up

Limited: a payment charged to an office, project or account whose allocation for the active period is used up waits for a person.

Every day at this time VENTHORA re-reads the connected sources and recalculates what each allocation has spent in the active period.

Vendorswho supplies goods and services, and the account they are paid into

VENTHORA pays a vendor only from this record. An invoice is trusted only from a registered mailbox; the money goes only to the bank account here; a new vendor, or a changed account, is not paid until a person calls back on the registered phone number (Payment → Payees). The payout method and currency decide the Airwallex corridor, the priority decides what is paid first when cash is short, and the budget line decides which budget it draws on.

Why every office, branch and unit is here
This tree is the organisation as VENTHORA sees it, and every payment is placed in it before it is paid:
Charge codes. Each payment carries OFFICE.PROJECT.ACCOUNT; the office part is the cost centre of a unit here. A code whose cost centre is not here, or not active, is never paid.
Budgets. Budget allocation follows this tree: a budget given to the head office covers every office under it; one given to a branch or unit covers that branch or unit only (Payment → Budget allocation).
Who approves. People belong to a unit (People and roles). An exception is routed to the approvers in the unit's chain whose approval limit covers it, nearest first; nobody approves outside their chain.
How much the agent pays alone. The payment limit of a unit (or of the nearest unit above it that sets one) is what VENTHORA may pay on its own for spending charged to that unit; above it a person approves.
Local currency and time zone. The currency an office is budgeted and reports in, and its local time; the reporting cut-off itself is one for the organisation (System Setting → Period, cut-off & sync).
Reports and segregation. Spending, payment reports and allocations are added up per office, branch and unit, and an admin of one office manages only that office and the units under it.

Optional: the time zone, the payment limit the agent may pay alone, an id of your own. Upload datasetany file, any layout: VENTHORA's agent reads it and files the data where it belongs

1 · What you do
  1. Choose one or more files: Excel, CSV, JSON, Word, PDF, HTML, an e-mail or plain text.
  2. Leave Auto-detect on, or pick the kind of data.
  3. Press Read the file(s), check the column matches, press Preview.
  4. Press Commit when the preview looks right.
2 · What VENTHORA does
  • Reads the file and decides what it holds: vendors, people, POs, budgets…
  • Matches each column to a VENTHORA field and cleans the values (amounts, dates, IBANs).
  • Preview: shows what would be created, updated or rejected, and why. Nothing is written yet.
  • Commit: writes the records through the same checks as entering them by hand.
3 · The result
  • Each record lands on its own page (Payees, Offices, Purchase orders…), with a link after Commit.
  • A row that fails a check is rejected with the reason; the rest go in.
  • A vendor's new or changed bank details still wait for a call-back before any payment.
  • Every commit is in the audit log: who, which file, how many records.
4 · Why use it
  • You: no API, no reformatting, no typing records one by one; the file as your old system exports it.
  • VENTHORA: starts on the company's real data instead of placeholders, checked on the way in.
5 · Limits and who reads the file
  • checking the AI model…
  • A one-time import, not a live link: upload again when the data changes, or use App Integration for a sync.
  • Photos, scans and old .xls files cannot be read; upload the PDF with text, .xlsx or CSV.
  • Up to 15 MB and 5,000 rows per file; Excel reads the first sheet that has data.
  • The file itself is not stored: it is held in memory until Commit, then only the committed records are kept.

How email reaches VENTHORAthree ways; use one or all of them

1 · A mail service pushes it
Suppliers write to, or copy, an address the company sets for VENTHORA. The mail service (Postmark, Mailgun, SendGrid, Cloudflare, …) posts each email the moment it arrives. Realtime.
2 · VENTHORA reads an inbox
Connect an existing mailbox (Gmail, Microsoft 365, Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, any IMAP). VENTHORA reads new mail on a schedule, every minute by default, and marks or moves what it read.
3 · Staff order payments by email
People ticked below may ask VENTHORA to pay a registered vendor by email. The order joins the queue and a different person approves it; email never pays by itself.

AI model

How the model is used

  • It reads emails, PDFs and the inbox, and proposes values with the words it read them from.
  • Code keeps a value only if that quote is in the source and the value is inside it; otherwise it is dropped.
  • When the model and the rules disagree, a person decides. It never sets an amount or moves money.
  • A hosted provider receives the email and PDF text, so it needs your confirmation first.
  • Keys are kept on this machine (git-ignored) and never shown again: only their last four characters.
  • Nothing saved here: MODEL_ENDPOINT / MODEL_NAME in .env apply.

Delegation of Authority (DoA)

What the FCO delegates to VENTHORA's agent, by kind of payment. Internal payments are the organisation's own recurring obligations: the FCO sets one standing payment instruction per cost category, and on the pay date the agent pays on its own what the instruction allows. External payments are invoices from vendors and short-term specialists. Anything outside the delegation is prepared for a person; some things are never executed. Every payment still passes the treasury limits and controls.
loading…
The mandate is the authority the finance team gives VENTHORA's agent. Inside it the agent checks, plans and pays on its own; outside it, it stops and asks a person. It has three parts: the checks every request must pass before anything is payable, the permissions and safeguards with their limits, and the approval policy for everything else. Every run on the Operations page starts from this standing mandate and records exactly what was granted. One role holds the authority to change it.
