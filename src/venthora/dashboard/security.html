Second factor checking…

A six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, asked for after your password. With it on, a stolen password alone cannot sign in as you. Recommended for everyone who can approve a payment.

  1. Open an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy).
  2. Add an account and choose enter a setup key. Type this key; it is shown only now:
